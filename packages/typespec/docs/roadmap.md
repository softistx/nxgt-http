# Roadmap

What `@nxgt/typespec` gives a spec, and what is coming. Each convention is the
shape the `@nxgt/*` packages already send, compiled to OpenAPI 3.1 and 3.2 and
generated by `@nxgt/openapi-codegen` in CI before it ships.

## Now

- **Verbs in operation ids.** In an interface `@operationIds` marks, a known
  verb takes the interface's resource: `list` in `Users` is `listUsers`,
  `create` is `createUser`, `findById` is `findUserById`, so a resource's
  operations are written once as `list`, `get`, `create`, and each
  interface names its own. The singular comes from a short English rule
  (`Categories`, `People`), and `singular`, `plural` and `verbs` options name
  what the rule does not know. Any other name, and any operation outside an
  interface, keeps its id as written; an operation named exactly a verb,
  or a verb followed by `By…` (`findById`), changes id, and keeps the old
  one with an `@operationId`; a `list` beside a `listUsers` now collides.

## Later

- **`ETag` and conditional requests.** `ETag`, with `If-None-Match` and
  `If-Match`, once the `@nxgt/*` packages send one the same way. The
  optimistic lock is the body's `version` today (`Versioned`).

## Shipped

### 0.4.0

- **Operation ids as written.** `@operationIds` names each operation
  exactly as the spec writes it, `findPostComments`, on an interface, or on a
  namespace to cover every operation in it, instead of
  `<operation><Interface>`. Ids are unique per service document.
- **No `Resource` template.** A resource's operations are written by hand,
  each with the name it has in the client; `SortParameters` stays, for the
  sort of a list.

### 0.3.0

- **Resource ids in the singular.** `Resource` named `read`, `create`,
  `update` and `delete` after its item, `readPost`, and `list` after the
  interface, `listPosts`.

### 0.2.0

- **One reply per status.** An operation that declares a reply without a
  body beside one with a body, such as `AuthenticationRequired` and
  `Unauthorized`, is an error, `duplicate-status-reply`, and two replies with
  a body of one status code a warning, `merged-status-reply`, instead of
  replies the emitter merges without a word.

### 0.1.0

- **Error replies.** The envelope `@nxgt/openapi-hono` sends, and the
  400, 401, 403, 404, 409, 422, 429 and 500 responses, with any other status
  through `ErrorResponse<Status>`.
- **Pagination.** `PageParameters` and `Page<Item>` for an offset page,
  `CursorPageParameters` and `CursorPage<Item>` for a cursor page, as
  `@nxgt/drizzle` and `@nxgt/mongo` page.
- **Authentication.** `JanusAuth`, the bearer, header and cookie schemes
  `@nxgt/janus` reads, and its guards' 401, 403 and 404 without a body.
- **Scalars and columns.** `uuid` and `email`, and `Timestamps`,
  `SoftDelete`, `Versioned` and `Actors`, the columns `@nxgt/drizzle`
  stamps a row with.
- **Headers.** `Idempotency-Key`, `Idempotent-Replayed`, the `RateLimit-*`
  headers and `Retry-After`, and the idempotent write's 409 and 422.
- **Resources.** `Resource<Item, Filters, SortField, Id>`: list, read,
  create, update and delete in one template, with typed filters and a sort
  by `orderBy` and `direction`.
- **Operation ids.** `@operationIds` names an interface's operations
  `listPosts`, `createPosts`, instead of `Posts_list`.

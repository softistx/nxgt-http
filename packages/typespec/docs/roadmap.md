# Roadmap

What `@nxgt/typespec` gives a spec, and what is coming. Each convention is the
shape the `@nxgt/*` packages already send, compiled to OpenAPI 3.1 and 3.2 and
generated by `@nxgt/openapi-codegen` in CI before it ships.

## Now

- **Error replies.** The envelope `@nxgt/openapi-hono` sends, and the
  400, 401, 403, 404, 409, 422, 429 and 500 responses, with any other status
  through `ErrorResponse<Status>`.
- **Pagination.** `PageParameters` and `Page<Item>` for an offset page,
  `CursorPageParameters` and `CursorPage<Item>` for a cursor page, as
  `@nxgt/drizzle` and `@nxgt/mongo` page.
- **Authentication.** `JanusAuth`, the bearer, header and cookie schemes
  `@nxgt/janus` reads, and its guards' 401, 403 and 404 without a body.
- **Scalars and columns.** `uuid` and `email`, and `Timestamps`,
  `SoftDelete`, `Versioned` and `Actors`, the columns `@nxgt/drizzle`
  stamps a row with.
- **Headers.** `Idempotency-Key`, `Idempotent-Replayed`, the `RateLimit-*`
  headers and `Retry-After`, and the idempotent write's 409 and 422.
- **Resources.** `Resource<Item, Filters, SortField, Id>`: list, read,
  create, update and delete in one template, with typed filters and a sort
  by `orderBy` and `direction`.
- **Operation ids.** `@operationIds` names an interface's operations
  `listPosts`, `createPosts`, instead of `Posts_list`.

## Next

- **One reply per status.** An error when an operation declares two replies
  of one status, such as `AuthenticationRequired` and `Unauthorized`, which
  the emitter merges without a warning today.

## Later

- **`ETag` and conditional requests.** `ETag`, with `If-None-Match` and
  `If-Match`, once the `@nxgt/*` packages send one the same way. The
  optimistic lock is the body's `version` today (`Versioned`).
